# Wayfarer

*The road is the destination.*

A cinematic driving game. Slide a pickup across desert dunes, climb an alpine pass in the snow, wind through countryside, redwoods, a red canyon and a northern fjord. Drive freely, race three rivals, chase your own ghost in a time trial, or score long drifts. Seven cars to earn, by day, dusk or night, in sun, fog, rain or snow.

**Play: https://eachmoon303030.github.io/wayfarer-web/**

Best in landscape. On a phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" to install it; it then works offline.
